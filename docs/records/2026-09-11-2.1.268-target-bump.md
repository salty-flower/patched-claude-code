### Minified-name reassignment

The 2.1.267 Darwin default-model getter used `ul()` and the session-effort selector used `rl(e,n)`.
In 2.1.268, the default-model getter moved to `dl()` while `ul(e,n)` became the session-effort selector.
A verbatim replacement port would therefore call the selector with no argument and read `e.sessionEffort` from `undefined` at render time.
The 2.1.268 model-effort entries bind `dl()` as the default-model getter and `ul(e,n)` only at the selector definition.
