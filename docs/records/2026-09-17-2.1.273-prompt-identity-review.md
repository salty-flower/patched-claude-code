### Duplicate occurrences

Byte-identical twins appear across the two graphs and are deliberately kept as
separate lineages: the gateway protocol doc (`0051`/`0052`), the host-asserted
permission context (`0114`/`0191`), the foreign-agent-config note
(`0293`/`0295`), and the six syntax-highlighting keyword tables
(`0163`–`0168` / `0371`–`0376`). Where a predecessor had one occurrence and the
current version has two, the pair is recorded as one `carry` plus one `split`.

`0114` and `0191` resolve by callsite, not text: `0114` sits beside lazy
per-event hook schemas (`.optional().describe(...)`) and carries
`prompt-000466`; `0191` sits beside the eager aggregate schema
(`.describe(...)`) and carries `prompt-000457`.

### Duplicate-site rule promoted

Two splits (`0092`, `0249`) and one merge (`0091`) were settled by the same
rule: one predecessor that appears at multiple current callsites produces one
`carry` plus `split` lineages for the additional occurrences; equal text at
distinct existing callsites does not merge those lineages.

The rule now lives in [Prompt Catalog](../guides/Prompt-Catalog.md#target-upgrade).

## Prior Lineages Without a Successor

52 lineages carried from 2.1.268 were triaged: 48 present in 2.1.273 but not
matched, 3 merged, 1 retired.

- **Retired**: `prompt-000377`, the `/ultrareview` posting-step agent prompt.
  Its work moved into deterministic comment formatting; the changelog records
  `/ultrareview --post` now posting directly.
- **Merged**: the Artifact "watching for republishes" family
  (`prompt-000496`/`000497`/`000498`) folds into `prompt-000415`/`prompt-000432`/
  `prompt-000126` — 2.1.268 carried the template twice and 2.1.273 keeps one copy.
- **Unmatched because of the scanner, not upstream removal**: the dominant cause
  is second-person → third-person rewrites dropping the `you must` / `do not`
  signals below the two-signal threshold. Two lineages (`prompt-000488`,
  `prompt-000499`) survive verbatim in 2.1.273 but produce no occurrence at all.

## Known Catalog Gaps

These are scanner findings, not lineage decisions, and are recorded here rather
than fixed in this bump:

- 12 of the 52 are not prompts: `prompt-000458`–`000463` and `prompt-000478`–
  `000483` are syntax-highlighting keyword tables captured by the candidate
  heuristic.
- Several `new` decisions are mechanically forced rather than semantic: their
  predecessors exist in 2.1.268 but were never catalogued — below the 160-char
  floor or short of two signals. Each rationale names the un-catalogued ancestor
  so a widened scanner can convert them to `carry` later. The same applies to
  `prompt-000415` in the 2.1.268 record.
