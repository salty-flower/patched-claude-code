# 2.1.285 Ultracode Decisions

## Model Availability Versus Effort Selection

**Status: approved by salty-flower on 2026-09-30.**
The maintainer approved the retirement batch and both equivalences bound by the digests below.
The [official 2.1.284 changelog](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog#2-1-284)
documents the independent Ultracode toggle and removal of forced xhigh effort.
The 2.1.285 command and UI treat ultracode as a workflow toggle independent of effort.
Enabling it preserves the selected effort, including `max` or `high`.
The model-availability predicate still rejects models without xhigh capability, including Opus 4.6.

Port the availability extension for capped max-capable models on both platforms.
Do not restore the previous coupling between enabling ultracode and selecting xhigh or max.
The target ledger records the digest-bound acknowledgement specified in [Patch Obligations](../rules/Patch-Obligations.md).
Historical patch entries remain unchanged and bounded to their original targets.

## Validation Boundary

For each platform, the new capability locator matches exactly once and its positive and negative static assertions pass.
An isolated harness executes the replacement with workflow, xhigh, max, and cap dependencies varied:
max accepted when allowed, capped max rejected, unsupported model rejected, workflow disabled rejected, existing xhigh accepted.
The harness also executes each staged command toggle with current effort `max` and `high`, confirming updates omit effort and messages retain the current value.
Staged settings-report functions pass active and inactive toggle checks.

These checks validate the semantic decision and replacement; they do not replace full `just verify`, rendered TUI checks, or obligation admission.
The audit supplied evidence; the subsequent maintainer approval supplies semantic authority.
