## Packaging compatibility

The expanded patch history exceeded the runtime's single-string capacity during release-manifest hashing.

Incremental hashing preserves the exact ordered names, NUL separators, repeated raw TOML, and intervening newlines,
without allocating that combined string.
Compatibility tests compare empty, single-entry, repeated-entry, and Unicode inputs against the previous algorithm.
